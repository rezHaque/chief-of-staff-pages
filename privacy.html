Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Chief of Staff is a personal AI assistant project. This policy explains how it handles data accessed through your Google Account.

What we access

With your authorization, Chief of Staff may access Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Sheets, and Google Docs data in order to perform actions on your behalf, such as reading and organizing information or managing tasks.

How data is used

Data accessed through Google APIs is used solely to operate this personal assistant for the account holder. It is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties, and it is not used for advertising purposes.

Data storage

Data is processed to fulfill the requested actions and is not retained longer than necessary for that purpose.

Revoking access

You can revoke this app's access to your Google Account at any time from your Google Account permissions page at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
